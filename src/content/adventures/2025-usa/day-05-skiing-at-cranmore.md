## Travel Log

With a horrendous weather forecast (rain and wind) we had bad timing for our pre-booked ski lessons at Cranmore.

Nonetheless we got kitted out and the kids had a ski leson and Nick had a free ski. Cranmore was actually really fun and the kids did amazingly considering the rain and this being the first time ever skiing on real snow.

Nick clocked up 25km of runs and a top speed of 44.4mph. Now his thighs hurt!

All our clothing is now completely soaked so it’s been quite the challenge to hang It up to dry.
