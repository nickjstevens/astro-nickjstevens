# Travel Log

Today we did the Broken Arrow Pink Jeep Tour- the iconic Pink Jeep crawled over rocks and inclines and slid down steep tracks. We saw beautiful rock formations and had a great guide who gave us a geology lesson and told us stories about the area. It was fascinating and a lot of fun.

After the tour we returned to the same ice cream place as yesterday for our favourite flavours. We then went over to [Tlaquepaque](https://www.google.com/url?q=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.tlaq.com%2F&sa=U&sqi=2&ved=2ahUKEwjWx4m4u_OHAxVHT0EAHYuuI2EQFnoECBkQAQ&usg=AOvVaw2lI5S4XO3Fp6GseXrdrvJK) village, a beautiful shaded artsy shopping district in Sedona. Here we wandered around the art galleries and then had lunch at the Secret Garden Cafe.

We spent the rest of the day relaxing again at our holiday house and pool, making the most of it before having to leave the following morning. The table tennis table got a lot of use as this is Jasper's favourite activity in the world.
